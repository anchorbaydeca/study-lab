Open the control panel

For your phone. Post announcements and reminders, mark departures and the lunch stop, update the coach's position and show the QR code.
  1. Laptop: connect HDMI to the coach's matrix and set the display to Extend.
  2. Display: open this page on the laptop, choose Open the display, move the window to the coach screens, then click it to go full screen.
  3. Control: open this same link on your phone and choose Open the control panel. Anything you post reaches the screens in a few seconds.
  4. Preview: run a sped-up demo of Thursday's drive to Louisville to see every screen.

      Start the coach display

      1. Connect the laptop to the coach's HDMI and set the display to Extend.
      2. Drag this window onto the coach screens.
      3. Click Start display. The page goes full screen. If it doesn't, use the browser's full screen (F11 on Windows, Control–Command–F on a Mac).
      4. On your phone, open this same link and choose Control panel to post announcements.

      Move the mouse at any time to bring back the Instructions and Control panel buttons in the top-right corner.
